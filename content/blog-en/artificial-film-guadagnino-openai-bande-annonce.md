The film about OpenAI's crisis now has a real trailer. On **November 18, 2026**, Neon released the full trailer for ***Artificial***, **Luca Guadagnino**'s film in which **Andrew Garfield** plays **Sam Altman**. It opens in US theaters on December 25.

If you make films with AI, it is a strange mirror: a film about AI, made in the most traditional way possible, with actors, sets and a studio that got cold feet about releasing it.

What follows comes from [ABC Audio's article](https://digital.abcaudio.com/news/andrew-garfield-sam-altman-artificial-official-trailer) (Mary Pat Thompson) and [Gizmodo's](https://gizmodo.com/new-artificial-trailer-gives-us-our-first-real-look-at-ike-barinholtz-as-elon-musk-2000841951) (Bruce Gil), both from November 18, and from [Variety's article](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/artificial-trailer-andrew-garfield-openai-biopic-1236802616/) of September 8 on the first trailer. I have not seen the film, only what the press describes.

## What Neon released

![ABC Audio article of November 18, 2026: Andrew Garfield is Sam Altman in 'Artificial' official trailer, with a still where Andrew Garfield and Yura Borisov raise glasses at a restaurant table](/images/blog/artificial-film-guadagnino-openai-bande-annonce/abc-audio-bande-annonce.webp)

*Source: ABC Audio, captured on November 19, 2026. Film still: Neon.*

*Artificial* is billed as a **comedic drama about the world of AI**. Guadagnino (*Call Me by Your Name*, *Challengers*) directs from a script by **Simon Rich**, a former *Saturday Night Live* writer. The film goes back to the 2023 period when Sam Altman was fired from OpenAI and brought back a few days later.

The official synopsis quoted by ABC Audio starts earlier: researcher **Geoffrey Hinton** makes a breakthrough, and Sam Altman, Elon Musk and "the most prominent figures in Silicon Valley" race to be part of it, then fight over who should control the technology.

The cast, according to Variety:

- **Andrew Garfield**: Sam Altman
- **Yura Borisov** (*Anora*): Ilya Sutskever
- **Monica Barbaro**: Mira Murati
- **Mark Rylance**: Geoffrey Hinton
- **Ike Barinholtz**: Elon Musk
- with Jason Schwartzman, Zosia Mamet, Cooper Hoffman, Chris O'Dowd, Cooper Koch and Billie Lourd

## What the trailer shows

According to the two November 18 articles, the trailer lines up a few key scenes:

- a **dinner** where Sutskever explains AI to Altman in plain words, before Altman orders two shots of vodka;
- the team discovering that its chatbot **can lie**. Mira Murati: "ChatGPT is generating misinformation. It can manipulate people and influence their behavior." Altman: "Mira, I'm aware.";
- according to Gizmodo, an Altman who says he is willing to lie for the greater good, and who ends up playing Candy Crush with a green juice, answering "Yeah, I know" when reminded of OpenAI's safety mission.

Gizmodo reads all this as a portrait of "a disinterested villain". Elon Musk only appears for a few seconds.

## A film Amazon did not want to release

![Variety article of September 8, 2026 by Mya Copeland: 'Artificial' Trailer: Andrew Garfield Is Sam Altman in Luca Guadagnino's OpenAI Movie as Neon Sets Christmas Day Release](/images/blog/artificial-film-guadagnino-openai-bande-annonce/variety-premiere-bande-annonce.webp)

*Source: Variety, captured on November 19, 2026.*

The film's path is almost as interesting as its subject. Amazon MGM was set to release it worldwide, then dropped it. In a June 19 statement quoted by ABC Audio, Amazon said the film "will be better served if it were released by a different studio". Variety notes the decision came after a major partnership between Amazon and OpenAI, including a 50 billion dollar investment. Still according to Variety, Netflix, A24 and Focus passed before **Neon** signed in June.

## What it tells people who make films with AI

Three things strike me, as a director who works with these tools every day.

**The real challenge is the explanation scene.** A dinner, two characters, one explains a language model to the other. It is the hardest scene to bring to life in a film about tech, and judging by the trailer, Rich and Guadagnino play it over a boozy dinner rather than in front of a whiteboard. If you are writing a short about AI, that is where it all happens. For the method, I covered [how to write a short film from A to Z](/en/blog/comment-ecrire-scenario-court-metrage-ia-a-z).

**AI scares distributors, even without a single generated frame.** There is no controversy about how the film was made: it was shot with actors. The problem came from business ties between a studio and an AI company. It is a useful reminder: distribution is still the weak link, all the more when your subject involves powerful companies.

**Filmmakers are taking the story back.** After [the anti-training notice in *The Odyssey*'s credits](/en/blog/nolan-odyssey-mention-anti-entrainement-ia), Hollywood is talking about AI its own way: by telling the story of the people who build it. *The Social Network* comes to mind. We will have to see the film to know if it holds up.

## What I would do this week

- **Watch the trailer as an exercise.** Mute it and watch how Guadagnino films people talking about something invisible: framing, looks, objects.
- **Note the structure.** A crisis over a few days, a confined setting, real characters. It is a format that also works for a short.
- **Keep December 25 in mind** if you follow awards season: Variety thinks it could make its way into the Oscar race.

## What we don't know

- **The French release date.** Neither Neon nor the press I read gives a distributor or date for France.
- **OpenAI's or Sam Altman's reaction.** I did not find a verifiable statement about this trailer.
- **The tone of the whole film.** A trailer picks its scenes. "Disinterested villain" is Gizmodo's reading, not necessarily the film's.
- **How long the crisis lasts on screen.** Variety says five days, Gizmodo four.

## FAQ

### What is the film Artificial about?

It is a comedic drama by Luca Guadagnino about OpenAI. It goes back to the 2023 period when Sam Altman was fired from the company and brought back a few days later, and to the race among Silicon Valley figures to control AI.

### Who plays Sam Altman in Artificial?

Andrew Garfield. Yura Borisov plays Ilya Sutskever, Monica Barbaro plays Mira Murati, Mark Rylance plays Geoffrey Hinton and Ike Barinholtz plays Elon Musk.

### When does Artificial come out?

In the US, in a limited release on December 25, 2026, then wider from January 8. No French date has been announced as far as I know.

### Who wrote the film?

The script is by Simon Rich, a former Saturday Night Live writer. Guadagnino directs and co-produces it with Simon Rich, David Heyman, Jeffrey Clifford and Jennifer Fox, according to Variety.

### Why did Amazon drop the film?

Amazon MGM said the film would "be better served" by another studio. Variety notes the decision followed a partnership between Amazon and OpenAI, including a 50 billion dollar investment. Neon picked up the film in June 2026.

### Was the film made with AI?

Nothing in the press says so. It is a film shot with actors that talks about AI without using it on screen, at least from what we know.

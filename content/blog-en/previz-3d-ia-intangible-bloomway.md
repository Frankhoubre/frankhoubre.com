You know the feeling: you write a twenty-line prompt for a tracking shot down an alley, you hit generate, and the camera goes somewhere else. You try again. Ten times. This week, two tools came out with the same answer to that problem: **you stop describing the shot and stage it in 3D, then the AI renders it.**

On **November 11, 2026**, Intangible launched **Intangible 2.0**. On **November 13**, Bloomway AI introduced its **3D Director Studio**. Two different teams, one method: the set in simple volumes, stand-ins instead of actors, a camera path, and only then, generation.

I read [Intangible's page](https://www.intangible.ai/), [the launch post by Bharat Vasan](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/bvasan_today-excited-to-launch-intangible-20-ugcPost-7526328043234062337-9d0V/), Intangible's co-founder, and [Bloomway's press release](https://manilatimes.net/2026/11/13/tmt-newswire/globenewswire/bloomway-ai-introduces-3d-director-studio-build-worlds-and-direct-ai-films-with-natural-language/2469362) as republished by the Manila Times, on November 14. I have not tested either tool yet.

## Intangible 2.0: one studio for image, video and 3D

![Intangible's home page, section Autopilot with hands-on control: four connected modes put AI to work, with complete creative control where it matters most, above views of a 3D island with palm trees and cameras](/images/blog/previz-3d-ia-intangible-bloomway/intangible.webp)

*Source: Intangible's website, captured on November 14, 2026.*

Intangible calls itself "one AI studio for images, video and 3D". According to its page, the tool has four connected modes:

- **Bring ideas and references together**, with AI agents coordinating the work.
- **Build the world** with AI, or import 3D and CAD assets.
- **Stage the scene**: the AI sets up the action and the shots, then you take hands-on control of cameras, framing, timing and movement.
- **Render** images and video with leading AI models.

In his launch post, Bharat Vasan describes a "semantic 3D harness": you talk to the tool like a creative director, and it translates that for image, video and world models. He claims **up to 10x fewer throwaway generations**. That is his number, not an independent measurement. Intangible gives **400 credits** at sign-up, and an MCP server lets you drive the canvas from Claude or ChatGPT. On its page, the company quotes users at Netflix and Amazon MGM who use it to plan stunts and virtual sets.

## Bloomway 3D Director Studio: previz for independents

![Bloomway AI's home page: What would you like to create today?, with a field to describe a project and thumbnails of video projects](/images/blog/previz-3d-ia-intangible-bloomway/bloomway.webp)

*Source: Bloomway AI's website, captured on November 14, 2026.*

Bloomway aims smaller: independent filmmakers, advertising creators, short-drama teams and educators, according to its release. The principle is the same, with more guidance:

1. You describe a place, for example "a rainy night in an Edo-era town, with a castle tower in the distance". The tool builds it in simple 3D, which you adjust in plain language.
2. You describe the action and the camera move. The tool turns them into an **animated previz** with stand-in figures.
3. You check the camera position, its path, the **focal length**, the **shot size** and the angle.
4. That previz is used as a reference for video generation, so the final shot follows your composition.

The release gives no price and no detailed access date.

## Why this is the right direction

![Diagram in three steps: the place, described then built in simple 3D; the blocking, with stand-ins, a camera path, a focal length and a shot size; the render, where the previz guides the video model so the shot follows your composition](/images/blog/previz-3d-ia-intangible-bloomway/schema-en.webp)

People often think the problem with AI video is image quality. On a film, the real problem is **control**: keeping the same set from shot to shot, putting the camera where you decided, cutting between angles. A prompt describes an intention. A 3D scene locks a geometry.

That is exactly what a cinematographer does with a floor plan: place the actors, the camera, the light, and the shoot follows. These tools put that step back before generation, instead of relying on luck.

> 💡 **Frank's take:** This is what I have been telling the people I train from the start: AI video does not replace directing, it demands it. Tools that ask you to place your camera before you generate are heading the right way. I am curious about Intangible for action scenes with several angles, and about Bloomway for teaching shot breakdown to beginners. ## What I would do this week

1. **Draw a floor plan by hand** for one scene of your project: the set from above, the characters, the camera and its path. Ten minutes, a pencil.
2. **Write your shot list** with the focal length and shot size of each shot. My piece on [the shot list for AI video](/en/blog/construire-liste-plans-shotlist-video-ia) gives you the template.
3. **Test Intangible with the 400 free credits** on that one scene, not on your whole film. Count the throwaway generations against your usual method.
4. **Keep the previz even if you switch tools.** A scene blocked in 3D is also a reference for your storyboard.

## FAQ

### What is Intangible 2.0?

An AI studio launched on November 11, 2026 that brings image, video and 3D together. You build a world in 3D, stage the action and cameras with the help of agents, then render the shots with image and video models. Sign-up gives 400 credits.

### What is Bloomway 3D Director Studio?

A tool introduced on November 13, 2026 by Bloomway AI. You describe a place and an action in plain language, it turns them into an animated 3D previz with stand-in figures, and that previz then guides video generation.

### What is previz?

Previsualization is a rough version of a scene, often in simple 3D, used to decide angles, camera moves and pacing before you shoot or generate.

### Why go through 3D instead of a prompt?

A prompt describes an intention, 3D locks a geometry. The set stays the same from shot to shot, and the camera is where you put it. The video model starts from your composition instead of inventing one.

### Do you need 3D skills to use these tools?

No, according to both companies. The set and the moves are described in plain language, and you then fix by hand what does not work.

### How much do Intangible and Bloomway cost?

Intangible gives 400 credits at sign-up, with no price grid on the page I read. Bloomway gives no price in its November 13 release.

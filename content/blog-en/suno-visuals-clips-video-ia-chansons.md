You have a song made with Suno, and you need a visual to post it. Since **November 17, 2026**, Suno lets you make it in the same place: **Visuals** turns a track into a video clip, a lyric video or album art.

The announcement comes from [Jack Brody's post](https://suno.com/blog/introducing-visuals), Suno's Chief Product Officer, published November 17. I read it with [the product page](https://suno.com/visuals) and two press articles, [Music Business Worldwide](https://musicbusinessworldwide.com/suno-launches-ai-music-video-tool-built-on-model-from-minimax-the-chinese-ai-firm-being-sued-by-disney-and-other-hollywood-studios) and [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/suno-visuals-ai-video-lyric-videos-album-art), on November 18. I have not tested the tool yet.

## What Suno launched

![Suno's post of November 17, 2026 by Jack Brody, Chief Product Officer: Introducing Visuals, with a mosaic of generated images, clay characters, plush toys, portraits and an astronaut](/images/blog/suno-visuals-clips-video-ia-chansons/suno-blog.webp)

*Source: Suno blog, captured on November 18, 2026.*

According to Suno, Visuals does three things:

- **Video clips** up to **15 seconds**, from a song dragged in from your library. Suno writes that the clips "follow your song's structure, pick up on your lyrics and lip sync right along with them". You can then extend, edit and re-prompt them with text.
- **Lyric videos** that move with the beat, with adjustable font and colors according to the product page.
- **Album art**, and clips made from a photo of your own.

To avoid the blank page, Suno provides house characters, or you upload your own image. Visual credits are **separate from song credits** and bought on their own. Suno says it is launching "today on web, iOS, and Android". Music Business Worldwide and The Next Web report the mobile apps coming in the next few weeks instead: check your app.

The model is not Suno's own. According to the product page FAQ, it is called **H3 Move** and is based on **MiniMax's H3**, tuned by Suno. Music Business Worldwide notes that MiniMax is being sued by Disney, Universal and Warner Bros. Discovery over images of their characters.

## The filters: what Suno refuses to do

This is the most interesting part for a creator. Suno announces text and visual filters that block uploads and prompts trying to depict **specific artists, public figures, copyrighted works and IP**. And the company owns it: prompts that work elsewhere "may not work on Suno and that's by design".

![Excerpt from Suno's post: new text and visual filters blocking prompts and uploads aimed at specific artists, public figures and copyrighted works, checks on generated videos before display, and ongoing work with Audible Magic, ACRCloud and Musixmatch](/images/blog/suno-visuals-clips-video-ia-chansons/suno-filtres.webp)

*Source: Suno blog, captured on November 18, 2026.*

The rest of the setup, again according to Suno:

- every generated video or image is checked **before** you see it;
- an **embedded watermark** is added before anything is shared off Suno;
- the first time you upload an image, Suno reminds you the tool is for original creation and asks you to confirm you have the rights.

## What it changes for a creator

For an independent musician, it is mostly a time saver on short formats. A 15-second clip timed to the chorus is exactly what a Reel or a Short needs. The claim that clips follow the structure and lyrics is the real thing to test: it is what is missing when you cut generic shots over a track.

For a full music video, it does not replace real directing. Fifteen seconds, even extended, is not a story. In my comparison of [AI tools for music videos](/en/blog/5-meilleurs-ia-video-maker-clip-musical-2026), the difference comes down to character consistency from shot to shot, and Suno says nothing about that.

The filters will annoy people who wanted "a clip with that singer's face". Good. A clip that imitates a famous artist is a legal problem you carry, not Suno.

## What I would do this week

1. **Test on the chorus first.** Take your best track, drag it into Visuals and generate a clip on the 15 seconds of the chorus. That is the part you will post anyway.
2. **Start from your own image** rather than a house character. A photo of you, your band or a drawing gives an identity other users will not have.
3. **Keep the song clean upstream.** A clip that follows the structure needs a clear structure: verse, chorus, bridge. My guide to [building a structured song with Suno](/en/blog/suno-udio-creer-chanson-structuree-a-z-ia) still applies.
4. **Check the price before generating in bulk.** Visual credits are separate, and Suno gives no price in its post.

## FAQ

### What is Suno Visuals?

It is Suno's new video and image tool, launched on November 17, 2026. It turns a song into a video clip of up to 15 seconds, a lyric video or album art. The clips follow the track's structure, pick up the lyrics and lip sync, according to Suno. You can then extend and edit them with text prompts.

### How much does Suno Visuals cost?

Suno gives no price in its announcement. Visual credits are separate from song credits and are bought on a dedicated page. According to the product page, usage depends on clip length and the quality level you pick (fast or standard). Pro and Premier subscribers get a limited number of Visual credits for a limited time.

### What model does Suno Visuals use?

According to Suno's FAQ, the model is called H3 Move. It is based on H3, the video model from Chinese company MiniMax, which Suno tuned and to which it added its own safeguards. MiniMax is separately being sued in the United States by Disney, Universal and Warner Bros. Discovery, according to Music Business Worldwide.

### Can I make a clip with a famous artist's face?

No, by design. Suno added text and visual filters that block prompts and uploads aimed at specific artists, public figures and copyrighted works. Suno warns that prompts that work on other video platforms may be refused on its own.

### Are Suno Visuals clips marked as AI?

Yes, according to Suno. An embedded watermark is added to visuals before they can be shared off the platform. Every video or image is also checked before it is shown, to catch content that breaks the community guidelines.

### Does it replace a real music video?

Not a full one. Fifteen seconds per generation is built for social media: a chorus, a loop, an animated cover. For a three-minute video with a story and consistent characters, you still need real directing and editing work, with or without AI.

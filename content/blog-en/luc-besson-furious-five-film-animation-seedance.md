**Luc Besson** is preparing an animated film, **Furious Five**, and according to Italian channel Sky TG24, it is generated with **Seedance 2.0**, ByteDance's video model. The director of *The Big Blue* and *The Fifth Element* mentioned it on 26 November 2026 at the Torino Film Festival, where he received a career award.

If it holds up, one of the best-known French directors in the world is turning to generative AI for a feature made for cinemas. For those of us who make films with these tools, that changes the conversation.

This is based on [Sky TG24's article](https://tg24.sky.it/spettacolo/cinema/2026/11/26/torino-film-festival-luc-besson-stella-della-mole) and [Cinematografo's](https://www.cinematografo.it/riflettori/luc-besson-non-sono-abituato-a-ricevere-premi-in-francia-a3ldot7j) (with the AdnKronos agency), both from 26 November, and on [World of Reel's write-up](https://www.worldofreel.com/blog/2026/11/26/luc-besson-has-directed-an-ai-generated-animated-movie-called-furious-five). All were read on 27 November 2026. Quotes are my translations from Italian. I haven't seen any footage from the film.

## What Besson said in Turin

![Cinematografo article of 26 November 2026: Luc Besson, "I'm not used to receiving awards in France", the director honoured with the Stella della Mole at the Torino Film Festival talks about Dogman, meeting Caleb Landry Jones and his new projects](/images/blog/luc-besson-furious-five-film-animation-seedance/cinematografo.webp)

*Source: Cinematografo, captured on 27 November 2026.*

Besson was receiving the Torino Film Festival's **Stella della Mole**. Speaking to the press, he talked about his career, his freedom as a producer and his projects. "I'm not used to receiving awards in my own country, in France," he said, according to Cinematografo.

On what's next, Cinematografo writes that he is working on an animated film called **Furious Five**, expected in cinemas **around mid-2027**. Sky TG24 goes further: Besson is reportedly finishing "an animated film generated with Seedance 2.0's AI," due in 2027.

![Excerpt from Cinematografo: Besson denied rumours of a new film shot in the United States; the project he is working on is an animated film called Furious Five, expected in cinemas by mid-2027](/images/blog/luc-besson-furious-five-film-animation-seedance/furious-five.webp)

One detail matters: **only Sky TG24 mentions AI**. Cinematografo, covering the same press meeting, describes an animated film without saying how it is made. World of Reel, which picked up the story in English, notes that it only exists in Italian press for now.

## "We were fifteen"

The line that struck me most isn't about AI. It's about his early days as a producer. "In Hollywood, in the studios, there are 2,500 of them. We were fifteen," he says in Cinematografo (Sky TG24 reports "two thousand"). He adds that there was no "committee of eighteen people" telling them which box a film should fit in.

![Excerpt from World of Reel of 26 November 2026: Luc Besson picked up a career award at the Torino Film Festival and let slip that he is working on an animated movie called Furious Five, made with Seedance 2.0, ByteDance's generative AI video model; the news so far only exists in Italian press](/images/blog/luc-besson-furious-five-film-animation-seedance/worldofreel.webp)

*Source: World of Reel, captured on 27 November 2026.*

He was talking about the past. But it is exactly what AI promises cinema: a small team doing what used to take an army. An animated feature usually means hundreds of animators and several years. If Besson is aiming for cinemas in 2027 with a video model, he thinks he can keep that team size.

## My take

I've been working with [Seedance 2](/en/blog/comment-obtenir-rendu-cinema-avec-seedance-2) for months. It's very strong on motion and light, which is why the news doesn't surprise me. What interests me is the choice of **animation**. It's where AI raises the fewest questions about actors, faces and image rights, and where a strong style hides small inconsistencies.

I stay careful on one point: a feature film isn't a string of beautiful shots. It's continuity, characters that stay the same for 90 minutes, editing, sound. What makes the difference isn't the model, it's the eye. Besson has one, which is why the project will be worth following, whether it works or not.

Then there's distribution. World of Reel points out that *The End*, Bruno Dumont's film made entirely with AI, still has no known distributor. Festivals are still hesitant. That's what I explained in [my approach to distributing an AI film](/en/blog/distribution-film-ia-strategies-visibilite-2026): releasing the film is only half the work.

## What it means for you

- **"It's not real cinema" carries less weight.** When a director of this stature uses the same model you do, your AI film gets judged on story and direction.
- **Think animation.** A drawn or stylised look forgives more than photorealism and avoids questions about real faces. It's good ground for a first short.
- **Keep a small team, but a complete one.** A writer, someone on sound, an editor: even with three people, those roles matter more than the number of generations.
- **Plan the release from day one.** AI-friendly festivals, platforms, audience: think about who will see your film before you finish it. The [real cost of an AI short film](/en/blog/combien-coute-court-metrage-ia-2026) includes that time too.

## What we don't know

- **Who produces and distributes** Furious Five.
- **The exact release date**: 2027 according to Sky TG24, mid-2027 according to Cinematografo.
- **What the film is about**, and whether actors provide voices.

## FAQ

### What is Furious Five?

Luc Besson's next film, an animated feature expected in cinemas around mid-2027, according to Cinematografo. Its story hasn't been revealed.

### Is the film made with AI?

According to Sky TG24, it's an animated film generated with Seedance 2.0, ByteDance's video model. Cinematografo doesn't mention AI. Besson hasn't detailed it publicly.

### Where did Besson talk about it?

At the Torino Film Festival on 26 November 2026, where he received the Stella della Mole for his career.

### What is Seedance 2.0?

A ByteDance video generation model that accepts text, images, audio and video as input and generates picture and sound together, according to its official page.

### When does the film come out?

In 2027 according to Sky TG24, around mid-year according to Cinematografo. No precise date.

### What does it change for an AI creator?

It makes the tool more legitimate with audiences and professionals. But a film is still judged on its story, its direction and its release, not on the model.

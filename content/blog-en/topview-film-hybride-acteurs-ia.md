Topview, which makes AI video tools, is billing the new version of its **Film Studio** as "the world's first hybrid film production platform". The idea fits in one sentence: you shoot **real actors**, then AI handles the lighting, camera angles, weather and extras. No character generated from scratch.

What interests me isn't the marketing superlative. It's the business bet behind it: according to Topview, fully AI films can't find distributors, and the only road to cinema that pays goes through flesh-and-blood actors.

This piece is based on [Jenny Priestley's article for TV Tech](https://tvtechnology.com/production/scripted-production/worlds-first-hybrid-film-production-platform-debuts), published around November 20, [Unite.ai's article on Video Relight](https://www.unite.ai/?p=524610) and [Topview's Film Studio page](https://www.topview.ai/film-studio), all read on November 22, 2026.

## What Topview announced

![TV Tech article by Jenny Priestley: World's First Hybrid Film Production Platform Debuts, a platform that combines live-action performances with AI-generated environments, shots and visual effects, illustrated by a grid of angles of the same driver at night: front, top-down, over the shoulder, eye close-up](/images/blog/topview-film-hybride-acteurs-ia/tvtech.webp)

According to TV Tech, the platform lets filmmakers **combine live-action performances with AI-generated environments, shots and visual effects**. Topview's page talks about "Film Studio 2.0", built for hybrid and AI-native production, with a 3D set builder.

The image that comes with the announcement sums up the promise: one take of an actor at the wheel, turned into ten angles. Front, top-down, over the shoulder, close-ups on the hand and the eye, a wide of the car in the street. On a traditional shoot, that's half a day of setups.

## The tools, one by one

![List of features in TV Tech's article: Video Relight to set the number of lights, their angle, colour temperature and intensity in 3D with a plaster-sphere preview, Video Element Edit, Camera Angle Presets to generate new angles from live-action footage, Add Extras for AI extras, Change Time and Weather for time of day, weather and moon phase](/images/blog/topview-film-hybride-acteurs-ia/outils.webp)

*Source: TV Tech, captured on November 22, 2026.*

- **Video Relight**: 3D lighting for video. You set the number of sources, their angle, colour temperature, distance, softness and intensity. A real-time **plaster-sphere** preview shows the result before you generate. Unite.ai dates its launch to November 19.
- **Video Element Edit**: retouch one element in the frame (by prompt, box select, click or brush) without touching the rest of the shot.
- **Camera Angle Presets**: generate new angles from live-action footage, so you can finish a scene **without going back to set**.
- **Add Extras**: add AI extras that you place one by one, choosing what each of them does.
- **Change Time and Weather**: switch day to night, change the weather, even the moon phase.
- TV Tech also mentions **green-screen replacement** and layer-based editing.

## The real story: money

Jensen Wu, Topview's co-founder and CEO, says it plainly in the announcement quoted by TV Tech: fully AI-generated films "have faced resistance from Hollywood" and have had limited access to mainstream distribution, with revenue "largely limited to YouTube". The hybrid model, which "combines real actors with AI enhancement", would make the work accessible to Hollywood's mainstream distribution channels and "better positioned to generate revenue."

That's a telling admission from an AI vendor. It matches what I see when I look at [how Chinese studios like iQiyi are using AI](/en/blog/iqiyi-studio-cinema-ia-shanghai): AI first replaces what's expensive around the actor (travel, reshoots, extras, waiting for the right light), not the actor.

## What it changes for you

If you make shorts, music videos or ads on a small budget, the hybrid approach is probably the most useful of both worlds. You keep what AI still often gets wrong, a look, a silence, a breath, and hand it what costs time.

- **Shoot clean, not perfect.** An actor well lit from the front against a neutral background leaves you every option. An underexposed exterior take, far fewer.
- **Be wary of generated angles.** A new angle has to invent what the camera didn't see: the back of a head, an off-screen set. Keep them for short inserts, not for a dialogue shot-reverse-shot.
- **Extras are the trap.** An AI crowd that repeats itself shows straight away. Topview says it worked on this, I haven't checked. To compare with what already exists, I went through the options in [generating visual effects with AI](/en/blog/generer-effets-speciaux-vfx-outils-ia).
- **Update the actor's contract.** If you alter a performer's take (lighting, angle, surroundings), tell them in writing before the shoot. The rights questions are covered in [my guide to AI actor clones](/en/blog/acteurs-ia-clones-droits-opportunites).

## What we don't know

- **The price.** Neither TV Tech nor the Topview page I read gives pricing for these tools.
- **How it holds up on a cinema screen.** The published examples are demos picked by Topview. Nothing shows how a generated angle holds up in 4K on a big screen.
- **The "world's first" claim.** That's Topview's wording. Other tools already offer relighting or set replacement, separately.
- **Which films use it.** No theatrically distributed production is named.

I haven't tested the platform: everything above describes what Topview announced.

## FAQ

### What is Topview Film Studio?

A Topview platform that combines live-action performances with AI-generated sets, shots and visual effects. Topview calls it the first hybrid film production platform.

### What is a hybrid film?

A film where actors are shot in live action, and AI changes or completes the rest: lighting, set, weather, camera angles, extras.

### What does Video Relight do?

It relights a video in 3D by setting the number of lights, their angle, colour, distance, softness and intensity, with a real-time preview on a plaster sphere.

### Why is Topview betting on real actors?

According to CEO Jensen Wu, fully AI films struggle to reach Hollywood distribution and mostly earn money on YouTube. The hybrid model is aimed at traditional channels.

### How much does Topview Film Studio cost?

Pricing isn't given in the announcement reported by TV Tech or on the page read on November 22, 2026.

### Can these tools replace a whole shoot?

No, and that's not the promise. The performance is still shot with real actors. AI works on what surrounds it.
